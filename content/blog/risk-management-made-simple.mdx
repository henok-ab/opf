Most failed evaluations come from breaking a drawdown limit, not from a lack of good ideas. Risk management is how you stay inside the rules long enough to hit the target.

## Know your limits first

Write down the daily loss limit and the maximum drawdown before your first trade, and note whether the drawdown is static or trailing.

## Size every trade from the stop

Decide where the trade is wrong, then size the position so that a loss there is a small, fixed share of the account.

## Keep a buffer

Treat your own stop for the day as lower than the firm's daily limit, so one bad trade never ends the account.
