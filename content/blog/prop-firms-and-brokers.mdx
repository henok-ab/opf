A prop firm funds you to trade. A broker holds your own money. Many traders use both, and each choice has its own checklist.

## Why use both

A prop account lets you trade the firm's capital with limited personal risk. A broker account is where you trade your own funds, so costs and regulation matter more.

## Choosing each

For the prop firm, start with the rules. For the broker, start with regulation and trading costs.
