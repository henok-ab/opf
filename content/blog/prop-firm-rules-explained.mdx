Prop firm rules look similar on the surface but differ in the details. This guide explains the common ones.

## Profit target

The gain you must reach to pass a phase. Some firms use one phase, some two.

## Daily and maximum drawdown

The daily limit caps how much you can lose in a day. The maximum drawdown caps total losses, and it is either static or trailing.

## Consistency and news rules

Some firms limit how much of your profit can come from one day, or restrict trading around high-impact news. Check each firm's current rules, because they change often.
