Choosing a prop firm comes down to a handful of rules. Compare them in the same order every time so no firm looks better than it is.

## What to compare

Look at the profit split, the number of phases, profit targets, daily and maximum drawdown, and whether drawdown is static or trailing. Then check the fees, payout rules and news-trading restrictions.
